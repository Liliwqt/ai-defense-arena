# Ticket 07 — Accepted voice delivery evidence

Local source: 6284f99 and 5c05207 on feature/question-first-room.
Baseline **64db4a7** was explicitly confirmed by the user. No push/deployment or
main change. This is code review and offline evidence, not user acceptance or
proof of actual recognition/coaching quality.

Only current server-finalized voice text matching an accepted original answer or
probe reply supplies capture provenance. The relay measures sample-clock recording
duration excluding server padding; client speed/pause claims are ignored. Speakers
retain separate original/reply evidence. Discarded/provisional/stale captures and
recognized clarifications are not accepted voice-performance records, while their
forwarded usage still counts under the existing shared cap.

Coaching cards distinguish exact captured hesitation/repetition examples, measured
recording facts and AI practice suggestions. Examples are bounded, context-dependent
observations; missing examples cannot prove filler-free speech. Ordinary
Bisaya/Tagalog particles are not automatically classified as fillers. Suggestions
preserve intended claims and paper facts, with conditional scaffolds for missing
information. Typed answers have no fabricated recording evidence, and unanswered
turns are not mislabeled as typed submissions.

The current gpt-live-transcribe pilot provides no word timestamps, transcription
confidence or server/semantic VAD. See the checked
[official transcription guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/realtime-transcription)
and [implementation evidence note](../../../docs/VOICE_DELIVERY_EVIDENCE.md).
Consequently speaking rate and linguistic pauses remain explicitly unavailable;
recording duration is a separate measurable fact, never actual speaking time.
Short, legacy and uncertain evidence gets an explanation, without invented zeros,
accent grades, psychological confidence or transcript-only tone/pronunciation claims.
Numeric speech measurements would need a separately validated source and quality
review. No provider/model substitution, extra evaluator or actual audio call was made.

English remains the only configured voice pilot. Its acknowledgment must match
before forwarding audio. Additional speech languages/code-switching require their
own representative quality evidence; typed language preferences are independent.
The existing pre-capture provider/app retention disclosure and no-audio-archive
policy remain. Overview, cards, inline typed Streamlit and exports preserve the
same evidence boundaries. No price, financial/schema or lifecycle rule changed.
