## Source and behavior

Source commit **2d637cc** on `feature/question-first-room` implements
[ticket 05](../issues/05-per-answer-text-coaching.md). The unchanged
[parent specification](../spec.md) remains canonical. Review comparison:
**6581ffe...2d637cc**. This is the actual ticket starting commit; the async
baseline-confirmation question remains unanswered. A different requested baseline
would require rerunning the review.

Research/mixed reports now include a required card for every recorded question,
including timeouts and asked questions ended before an answer. The server copies
original words, actual speakers, citations and available provenance from captured
transcript data; AI suggestions cannot replace them. Actual probe replies receive
separate coaching for their own speaker. Unasked future questions get no cards.

Each card contains specific strengths/improvements, stronger suggested wording
and a practice task. Missing facts are labeled conditional scaffolds. Unanswered
originals have factual guidance, no strength and no fabricated stronger answer.
Wording examples must occur verbatim in the matching contribution. The request
receives the confirmed research profile, approaches, stage, setting and evidence;
grounding instructions prohibit invented methods, safeguards or findings.
Structural validation does not prove the truth or usefulness of generated prose.

The additive contract preserves legacy/code-only reports. Results uses an
accessible question selector and one focused card with expandable original and
improvement sections. The complete transcript remains available separately.
Typed Streamlit shares the cards; downloads include completed coaching, original
contributions, separate replies/provenance and truthful report status. Typed
input has no audio measures; voice recognition/duration alone is not evidence of
pace, pauses or spoken filler totals. Reliable voice feedback remains ticket 07.

This slice uses the existing complete-report request, optional closing and
whole-report retry. The boundary is **12 recorded turns**, bounded fields and a
**64,000-character serialized report**. Longer, missing, duplicate, oversized or
invalid reports fail recoverably before ready status or successful settlement.
Empty discussions get factual guidance without AI. Ticket 06 adds long-report
batching, progressive Results and failed-section-only retries; none is claimed
here. See [operator notes](../../../docs/RESEARCH_COACHING.md).
