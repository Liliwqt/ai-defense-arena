[Ticket 02](../issues/02-repeated-opening-host-choice.md) and the unchanged
[parent specification](../spec.md), stories 20–25, define scope. Ticket 01's
research-first setup/history remains in place. Voice and detailed answer coaching
are later tickets and are not implemented by this change.

After bounded duplicate generation, the host sees the candidate and exact source
as **Preview · not active**, with Retry opening and Accept repeated question.
Guests see waiting guidance without the private candidate or host controls.
Nothing becomes an asked question and no vote/answer clock starts until acceptance
commits successfully. Retry consumes the existing finite attempt allowance.

A random candidate identity is bound server-side to the current room, host, run,
map, generation and accepted paper. Restart/end/shutdown invalidate it. Acceptance
rechecks the current session, source and private history and uses the existing
atomic charge/history transaction with explicit repeat permission. Concurrent
replays cannot double-charge or append another opening. Failed persistence keeps
the candidate recoverable. Map changes are rejected while waiting; old choices
are rejected after restart. No prices, answer timers or code-only flow changed.
