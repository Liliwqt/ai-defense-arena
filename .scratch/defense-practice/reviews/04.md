Date: 2026-10-18. Status: implemented and independently code-reviewed locally;

## Source and behavior

User-confirmed baseline: **0b9893b**. Source commit **daf9cd7** on
`feature/question-first-room` implements [ticket 04](../issues/04-voice-probes-and-recovery.md),
using the unchanged [parent specification](../spec.md).

The chosen defender can record a same-question probe reply with its own probe
and attempt identity. The question number, existing reply deadline and answer
limits are unchanged. Speaker desk uses Spoken reply, Reply time and Send voice
reply. Original answers and probe replies retain separate speaker attribution
and input provenance, including in Transcript and summary exports. Team chat
and explicit clarification controls retain typing.

A finalized unsent draft remains privately in bounded room memory only while
its player, run, view, question, probe and interpretation identity remain valid.
Navigating away, backgrounding or disconnecting stops capture and clears Send
intent. Returning never restarts the microphone or replays Send. A valid final
draft can be reviewed read-only and sent by a fresh manual action. Provisional
words are not restored into typing. Existing typed drafts require explicit
replacement before voice restoration; Switch to typing opens an empty answer.
Discard revokes the matching proof, without refunding consumed voice allowance.
No new voice-draft persistence in browser storage or audio archive is added.

Interpretation failure/retry, saved-answer acceptance and direct replacement
retain the existing recovery rules. Provenance attaches only to the matching
server-finalized original or probe text; a new typed answer does not inherit it.
A recognized clarification is not recorded as an answered turn. New submissions,
probe/selection/run changes and lifecycle invalidation reject obsolete proofs.
Original answers, probe replies, discarded recordings and retries share the
existing server-counted allowance. Prices, voucher rules and database schema
are unchanged. See the [operator guide](../../../docs/LIVE_VOICE_PILOT.md).
