# Ticket 06 — Progressive research coaching and safe completion

Local implementation: f679c8f and caa0671 on feature/question-first-room.
Review baseline: **3f340ca**, explicitly confirmed by the user. No push,
deployment or main change. This is a local code-review handoff; user visual
acceptance and actual provider quality remain pending.

Research/mixed coaching freezes the accepted transcript, source and profile.
One overview and sequential groups of up to three questions use the existing
finite admission limits. Stable job ownership and coordinator lifecycle fences
prevent duplicate/stale work from changing another report. The supported room
budget is 100 recorded questions. Batching can add requests/cost; this is not the
provider's discounted Batch API.

The optional closing appears once after the overview, retaining the server's
remaining interval on reconnect. Ready cards open progressively in Results;
Account/payment tasks stay open. The question selector reaches pending/failed
questions honestly, while ready originals, sources, speakers, probe contributions
and provenance stay intact. Recovery targets failed sections only. Completion-write
retry reuses every accepted section without another AI request or defense charge.

Discussion completion and visible Results do not settle the run. Every required
section must validate, then the durable completion transaction must return the
correct terminal outcome. Partial jobs do not expire as successful rooms. Existing
eligible unavailable-run recovery returns paid credits once; free/voucher runs
create no award. Return, abandonment, restart and shutdown fence obsolete work.
Exports retain originals, attribution, progress/status and ready coaching. No
Results/audio archive is added. Code-only keeps its single-report flow.

Typed Streamlit uses the shared bounded membership/validation/retry state. It
renders accepted cards and the inline closing before advancing another request,
retains ready work on failure, and never invokes live-store compensation.
