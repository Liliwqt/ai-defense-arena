[Ticket 03](../issues/03-live-voice-answers.md) and the unchanged
[parent specification](../spec.md) define the slice. The chosen research/mixed
defender explicitly starts capture, sees read-only recognized words and manually
sends a server-finalized answer through the existing acknowledgment path.
Silence, final events and the shared ten-minute allowance never submit it.
Typed drafts require an explicit replacement decision; switching back opens a
fresh editable answer. Probe replies remain typed until ticket 04. Native voice,
code-only voice and richer delivery coaching are outside this ticket.

The coordinator owns finite speech attempts bound to player, room/run, turn and
view. Server-side authorization, deadline and finalized-text checks reject forged
or stale input. Audio forwarding, segments, provider buffers, request rate,
concurrent streams, attempts and finalization time are bounded. Navigation,
backgrounding, disconnect and room lifecycle changes revoke capture/work;
blocked provider IO cannot hold the room lock. Retries and discarded audio still
consume the shared allowance. Bounded short-tail padding also consumes allowance
and is recorded separately. Extra in-flight frames after the cap are discarded
without losing valid buffered finalization.

Only accepted transcript/provenance is retained with the answer. No app audio
archive or voice draft browser persistence is added. Standard speech credentials
stay server-only, separate from text AI. Price, voucher rules, payment storage
and database schema are unchanged. See the [operator pilot guide](../../../docs/LIVE_VOICE_PILOT.md)
for configuration, disclosure and validation boundaries.
