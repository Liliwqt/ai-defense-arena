with member-only operation/run/job/section identities. Context is Team overview,
public question numbers or completed-section identity, never pending advice.
Finished sections retire their highlights immediately; ready cards remain visible.
Queued gaps use factual static labels. Missing or stale job metadata falls back
without trusting unverifiable context. Completion/failure/cancellation clears activity.

for mapping/remapping, question/answer/clarification/probe work and coaching.

removes the native subtree. Manual map, saved-submission, question and coaching
retries say Trying again; ordinary remapping and fresh subsequent stages do not.
