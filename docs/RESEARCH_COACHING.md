# Research answer coaching — local checkpoint

Research and mixed defenses now require a validated coaching card for every
recorded question, including a factual card for a missed answer or an asked
question ended before submission. Unasked future questions do not receive cards.
Code-only and older reports retain their existing summary format.

The server copies original words, speakers, citations and available input
provenance from the accepted transcript. AI suggestions cannot replace those
originals. An actual probe reply receives separate feedback for its own speaker;
a missed probe is not fabricated. Research profile, stage, setting, paper evidence
and accepted discussion remain context for the existing coaching request.

Each card contains specific strengths and improvements, suggested wording and a
practice task. Missing facts use a labeled conditional scaffold. Unanswered
originals have no strength or invented stronger answer. Wording examples must
occur verbatim in the matching contribution. Typed input has no audio measures;
recognized voice text and forwarded duration alone do not establish filler totals,
speaking rate, pauses, accent, tone, pronunciation or psychological confidence.
These instructions and structural checks do not prove live model coaching quality.

Results uses a question selector with focused, expandable original/improvement
sections. The full transcript remains available; summary downloads retain
accepted originals, separate replies/provenance, completed coaching and status.
The typed Streamlit fallback shares the card contract without voice or financial
compensation features.

Ticket 05 keeps complete reports in one bounded request: at most twelve recorded
turns, bounded field/card/overview lengths and a 64,000-character serialized report
limit. Longer, missing, truncated, duplicate or invalid reports fail recoverably;
they do not publish ready or settle the defense successfully. Existing admitted
whole-report retry, optional closing and eligible unavailable-run recovery remain.
Empty discussions produce factual guidance without a model request. Ticket 06
adds long-report batching, progressive cards and failed-section-only recovery;
this checkpoint does not claim those features.

Offline contracts, mocked authenticated defenses and synthetic browser checks are
separate from actual model quality, hosted/provider and installed-device evidence.
No real AI/speech/payment call or deployment is authorized by this document.
