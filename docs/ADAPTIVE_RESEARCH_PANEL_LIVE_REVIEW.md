An explicit request for modern Bisaya produced a same-question explanation, followed by Bisaya questions, reactions and coaching. The selected defender corrected an assumption about six-month deletion; the next reaction recognized that the checklist was a proposal, not something demonstrated in the paper. Impact accepted preliminary design observations without claiming causality, while Critical asked about familiarity effects despite counterbalanced task order.

> Klaro nga proposal pa ang checklist, dili pa usa ka proseso nga naa sa paper.

This reaction follows the defender's actual correction. Most wording was concise and included familiar English research terms, but some phrasing remained formal. Native Cebuano user review is still needed; language continuity is observed, not native-language perfection.

**Theoretical.** Methodology asked how arguments and counterexamples would test the fairness claim. A vague answer prompted one grounded request to distinguish a counterexample requiring revision from a disclosed exception. The fuller reply was accepted and the panel advanced. Ethics asked about explicit value premises and charitable treatment of opposing arguments, without inventing participants or demanding consent. Impact recognized the value of conceptual distinctions without commercial/service improvement. Critical asked how relevant similarity would be defined, rather than imposing statistical tests.

> How will you make the paper’s value assumptions explicit while representing opposing arguments charitably?

**Interdisciplinary.** Initial mapping kept qualitative interviews separate from computational/design work, explicitly declining to infer mixed-methods design merely from multiple components. The panel then asked about count validation and freshness using the actual code comment, including its indentation.

## Findings and refinements

1. **Planned-step tense:** the empirical Ethics question mentioned planned deletion, then used wording that could imply it had already happened. The defender corrected the timing and the panel accepted it. Shared research guidance now explicitly asks about future steps in future/conditional terms, including in completed studies, or checks whether they occurred when unclear.
2. **Coverage versus question labels:** empirical coaching said “Q1 ug Q5–Q7” while describing covered topic IDs, although the defense had only four questions. Its structured turn references and detailed Q1–Q4 points were valid. Shared research guidance now directs coaching to name coverage by topic title and never convert a topic ID into a defense question number or call an unasked topic an unanswered question.
3. **Occasional invalid provider reference:** one mapping attempt was rejected and recovered safely, as described above. This remains a possible provider-output failure, not evidence of universal mapping reliability. Citation validation and manual mapping retry remain unchanged.

The two refinements add five prompt lines inside the existing research-only boundary. They change no AI schema, request count, timer, coverage/budget, credit rule or code-only guidance. They are prompt safeguards, not deterministic guarantees.
